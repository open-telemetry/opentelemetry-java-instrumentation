The experimental Kafka broker JMX metrics now start with the JMX domain of their MBean, use
singular count units (for example `{message}` instead of `{messages}`), and report durations in
seconds. For dashboard and alert updates, `kafka.network.io`, `kafka.purgatory.size`,
`kafka.partition.count` and `kafka.lag.max` are prefixed with `kafka.server.`; `kafka.message.count`,
`kafka.request.count`, `kafka.request.failed`, `kafka.isr.operation.count` and
`kafka.partition.underReplicated` become `kafka.server.messages`, `kafka.server.requests`,
`kafka.server.failed_requests`, `kafka.server.isr.operations` and
`kafka.server.partition.under_replicated`, respectively. `kafka.partition.offline` becomes
`kafka.controller.partition.offline`, while `kafka.leaderElection.count` and
`kafka.leaderElection.unclean.count` become `kafka.controller.leader_elections` and
`kafka.controller.unclean_leader_elections`. `kafka.request.queue` becomes
`kafka.network.request.queue`; `kafka.request.time.total` becomes `kafka.network.requests` because
it reported a request count, while `kafka.request.time.50p` and `.99p` become
`kafka.network.request.duration.p50` and `.p99` in seconds instead of milliseconds.
`kafka.logs.flush.Count` becomes `kafka.log.flushes`, and `kafka.logs.flush.time.50p` and `.99p`
become `kafka.log.flush.duration.p50` and `.p99` in seconds instead of milliseconds.

The attributes `type`, `direction` and `operation` become `kafka.request.type`,
`network.io.direction` (`in` and `out` become `receive` and `transmit`) and
`kafka.server.isr.operation`; request type values are now lowercase. The metrics
`kafka.server.messages`, `kafka.server.requests`, `kafka.server.failed_requests` and
`kafka.server.network.io` now have a `kafka.topic.name` attribute instead of a broker-wide value.
`kafka.server.isr.operations` is now a counter instead of an updowncounter.
