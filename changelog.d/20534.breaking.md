Remove `otel.instrumentation.couchbase.experimental-span-attributes`. Use
`otel.instrumentation.couchbase.emit-experimental-telemetry=true` to enable experimental span
attributes for Couchbase 2.x and 3.x. For Couchbase 3.x, this also enables the Couchbase Java SDK's
internal `request_encoding` and `dispatch_to_server` spans.
