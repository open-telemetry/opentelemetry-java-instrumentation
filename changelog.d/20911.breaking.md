Configured service mappings now emit `server.service.name` and `server.service.namespace` instead of
`peer.service`, `service.peer.name`, and `service.peer.namespace`. Rename
`otel.instrumentation.common.peer-service-mapping` to
`otel.instrumentation.common.server-service-mapping`; in declarative configuration, use
`java.common.server_service_mapping` with `target` instead of `peer`.
